# Translation guide · Interpretation discipline

## Levels

**Original → literal Chinese → explanatory paraphrase → philosophical reflection.** The current V1 data exposes original and paraphrase only. Literal alignment is a future project.

## Core vocabulary

| Term | Cautious first gloss | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 道 | way, process, guiding principle, generative source (contextual) | A single modern physical law |
| 德 | efficacy / virtue / realized potency | Only present-day moral praise |
| 无为 | acting without contrived coercion or excessive intervention | Complete inactivity |
| 自然 | what is so of itself | Simply modern environmental conservation |
| 有 / 无 | being / absence, formed / unformed, context-dependent | Treating every use as identical |
| 玄 | deep, obscure, subtle | Certainty about supernatural powers |
| 柔弱 | softness, yieldingness, adaptable strength | Weakness as a universal virtue |
| 不争 | avoiding competitive assertion, non-contention | Renouncing all legitimate goals |
| 朴 | uncarved simplicity | Lack of all learning |
| 慈、俭 | care and restraint | Anachronistic policy formula |

## Editing discipline

- Keep original Chinese punctuation and translation clearly separated.
- Preserve metaphors (water, valley, infant, bow, wheel-hub) before abstracting them.
- If a line has substantial alternative interpretations, label one as "a reading" and describe the other.
- For chapter 5, avoid equating 刍狗 with contempt for people without scholarly context.
- For chapter 65, do not sanitize "愚民" into an uncontested progressive pedagogy.
- For chapter 81, 知者不博 need not mean knowledge is undesirable.
- Cite when making provenance, manuscript ordering, palaeography or disputed lexical claims.
- Don't copy modern translations wholesale.

## Example, chapter 11

Original: 三十辐共一毂，当其无，有车之用。  
Close: Thirty spokes meet at one hub; its empty center allows the cart to function.  
Commentary: "Use" depends partly on absence; do not erase the material spokes. This is an illustration, not a modern theory of physics.

## Disagreement policy

Different editions and commentators are allowed to disagree. Name the edition, cite the passage, indicate uncertainty, and do not force a false consensus.
