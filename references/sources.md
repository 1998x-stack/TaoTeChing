## Version review checklist

When changing original text:
- Identify a fixed bibliographic or revision anchor.
- Copy exact before / after readings.
- Separate witness readings from editorial emendations and punctuation.
- Identify modern simplified conversion and its reversibility risks.
- Confirm passage placement rather than assume 81-chapter correspondence.
- Record reviewer(s) and unresolved ambiguity.

High-interest investigation areas: chapter 1 常/恒, chapter 38 De opening in manuscript ordering, chapter 41 variants of 大器, chapter 64 analogies, chapter 65 愚之.
